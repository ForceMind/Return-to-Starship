## Translation and synchronization policy

- Begin full English literary translation only after the corresponding Chinese chapter has completed editorial review and received the author's approval. The Chinese novel's provisional D-route plot should not be mistaken for historically confirmed author canon.
- Keep both languages aligned by ID: `zh/00-introduction.md` ↔ `en/00-introduction.md`, and `zh/NN-chapter.md` ↔ `en/NN-chapter.md` for 01–22.
- Record which **approved Chinese commit/blob SHA** each English chapter translates. If the approved Chinese source changes, flag the English counterpart as needing synchronization rather than silently updating it.
- Preserve the author's scene chronology, named entities, speculative-science uncertainty, character knowledge boundaries, and narrative tension. Use natural literary English rather than word-for-word conversion.
- Alien names and other specialized terms have **provisional Roman spellings** in [the translation glossary](../../docs/translation-glossary.md); verify before a full English edition is frozen.
- Publisher/author release approval is separate from a chapter file's existence and separate from the GitHub repository's visibility.
